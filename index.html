Hi, I'm Songtao Mao. I'm currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Prof. Xin Li. Prior to this, I earned my Bachelor of Science degree in Mathematics from Zhiyuan College at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My primary research focus is on theoretical computer science. Previously, I worked on pseudorandomness and coding theory. More recently, my research interests have shifted toward average-case complexity, cryptographic hardness, and the theory of AI safety.
